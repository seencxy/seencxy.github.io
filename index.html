Anti-bot engineering

Notes on bot defense and the cookies that decide whether you pass

I write about the parts of anti-bot systems that are documented nowhere: what _abck actually stores, why PerimeterX _px3 has two clocks, why Akamai's ApiURL matters more than cookie freshness, and the handful of checks that separate a real session from a replayed one.

Everything here is written for QA automation and integration testing on properties you own or are authorised to test.
